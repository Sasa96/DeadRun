**Description:**
It's 2:47 AM. The emergency alert says stay inside. Then the glass cracks.

Dead Run is a 3D zombie-escape runner. Tilt your phone to steer, swipe to jump and slide, and turn at every corner before the street becomes a dead end — the horde is never far behind.

ESCAPE THE CITY
• Ten-mission campaign: leave your neighborhood, reach Precinct 9, restore power at St. Agnes Hospital, rescue the Ward C survivors and make it to the last boat out
• Endless mode through three districts: ruined city streets, the police station and the hospital
• Infected office workers, police officers, doctors and nurses walk straight at you — read the lanes and slip past

RUN SMART
• Smooth tilt steering with calibration and adjustable sensitivity, or touch controls
• Jump sinkholes and barricades, slide under quarantine gates
• Shields, torches and flares buy you seconds when you need them most
• Clip something while the horde is far back and you stumble; clip it while they're close and it's over

KEEP GOING
• Threat levels rise from Calm to Dead Run the further you get
• Earn trophies for distance, corners taken and supplies found
• Caught? Watch an optional ad for one second chance per run

How far will you get before they catch you?
