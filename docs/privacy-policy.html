Dead Run Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

This policy explains what information the Dead Run mobile game ("the app") handles, and why. Dead Run is published by Fairsmith ("we", "us"). The short version: we do not run accounts or servers, and the game itself keeps your progress only on your device. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google collects some information to serve and measure those ads.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (best distance, run statistics, campaign progress, trophies) and settings (control mode, tilt sensitivity, sound preferences) are saved locally on your device. This data is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

If you choose tilt steering, the app reads your phone's motion sensor while you play to steer your character. These readings are used instantly on your device and are never stored or transmitted.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app offers an optional rewarded video ad: after a run ends, you can choose to watch an ad to continue once. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, including the Apple Advertising Identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking when the app asks
  • IP address, and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language)
  • Ad interactions (ads shown, views, taps) and diagnostic data (crashes, performance)

Google uses this information to deliver ads, measure their performance, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud. If you allow tracking, ads may be personalized to your interests. How Google uses this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • App Tracking Transparency (iOS): the app asks your permission before your device's advertising identifier can be used for tracking. If you decline, you still get ads, but they are not personalized using that identifier. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: if you are in these regions, the app shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested, where you can accept or refuse personalized advertising.
  • Watching ads is optional: the game is fully playable without ever watching an ad.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or ask for your name, email or contacts.
  • We do not operate servers that receive your data, and we do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use your camera, microphone, photos or precise location.

Children

Dead Run is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention and your rights

Data saved by the game stays on your device until you delete the app. Information collected by Google is kept according to Google's own retention policies. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your personal information; for advertising data, you can exercise these through your device's tracking and advertising settings and through Google's privacy tools.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
