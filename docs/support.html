Common questions

  • Tilt steering drifts or feels off. Pause the run, hold the phone the way you play, and tap Calibrate. You can also adjust sensitivity there, or switch to touch controls.
  • Motion access was denied. Enable it in Settings → Dead Run → Motion & Fitness, or use touch controls.
  • I don't see the "Second chance?" offer. It appears only when an ad is available, once per run. The game is fully playable without it.
  • Is my progress backed up? Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes it.
